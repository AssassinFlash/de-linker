Before you order

Commercial and trade rules.

Ordering & delivery

What is the minimum order quantity, and where does Linker deliver?

There is no pallet minimum. Orders start from individual units, delivered across the EU. Equipment ships according to project schedules, with trade quotes detailing destination freight.

Can different brands be combined in one project order?

Yes. Inverters, batteries, smart meters, and accessories from different brands can be combined in one order. They ship together under a unified invoice; check each pairing against the compatibility lists on file before ordering.

Does Linker supply complete storage packages or individual components?

Both. Installers can order packaged systems with matching balance-of-system components, or source individual replacement units and accessories.

How are project quotations processed?

Quotations include confirmed B2B net pricing, a full bill of materials, and applicable freight terms for the project destination.

Accounts & pricing

How do trade partners access net pricing?

Wholesale net pricing is visible to verified partner accounts. Unregistered contractors can submit project parameters to receive a formal trade quotation.

Who is eligible for a Partner Account?

Professional solar installers, electrical contractors (Elektrofachbetriebe), and EPCs operating within the EU. Registrations are reviewed individually before account activation.

Shipping & logistics

How are lithium battery systems transported?

Batteries are classified as dangerous goods (Class 9) and transported via certified freight carriers. Systems carry UN38.3 transport certification, with compliant crating and documentation managed by Linker.

What is the procedure if transport damage occurs?

Note visible packaging or pallet damage on the freight receipt (CMR) at delivery. Linker coordinates carrier claims and replacement units to avoid project downtime.

What delivery destinations are supported?

Deliveries route directly to your company warehouse, branch location, or scheduled project sites across the European Union.

Warranty & service

Whose warranty applies to the supplied equipment?

Statutory B2B warranty rights apply, and product guarantees apply as issued in the documents on the product pages and in this library. Linker handles any claim for you, from your order.

How are warranty claims processed?

Open Warranty & Replacements from your order. The signed-in account confirms the order, and the form keeps the serial number, model, and fault description with that order. Linker handles the diagnostic exchange with the manufacturer's technical desk. Open Warranty & Replacements.

What technical support is included with an order?

Linker provides sizing tools and wiring references before the order, and live commissioning troubleshooting from the engineering desk in Germany.

Compliance & documentation

Is equipment certified for the German electrical grid?

Grid compliance is documented per model: inverters for low-voltage connection need a Unit Certificate (Einheitenzertifikat) and NA-Schutz documentation under VDE-AR-N 4105, medium-voltage systems follow VDE-AR-N 4110. Linker requests the certificates for the models in your order.

Are documentation and manuals available in German?

Datasheets and manuals are on file in English, and in German where a German edition exists. Linker's engineering desk helps with technical questions on any of them.

Are Declarations of Conformity provided with orders?

Yes, on request: Linker requests the CE Declarations of Conformity and relevant test certificates for the models in your order.

What technical documentation is available through Genie?

Genie indexes the datasheets, manuals, and compatibility matrices in this library, retrieving specific technical parameters and wiring requirements directly.
