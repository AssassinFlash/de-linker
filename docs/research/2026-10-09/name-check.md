### 2.4 What this means
- Using "Genie" as the name of Linker's own assistant on de-linker.com, always beside the house mark "Linker" and an AI descriptor, is low-risk: the plain-GENIE owners use it for unrelated goods (UK car telematics, calculators, garage doors, hearing aids, genomics) or carved e-commerce/online retail out (TikTok, Genie AI Ltd), and the Austrian energy-sector holder has been bankrupt since 2019.
- Paper exposure worth knowing: AA's EUTM 016456171 class 35 (online retail of electrical apparatus/batteries + consumer advice) and GENIE GmbH's DPMA mark (class 9 software). Legal inference, not verified: both registrations are past the 5-year use grace period, so they are exposed to non-use defences for goods/services not actually used; 016456171 is due for renewal on 2027-03-10.
- **Do not file "GENIE" (alone or as "LINKER GENIE")**: earlier identical marks in 9/35/42 would face it, and the word is weak for AI helpers. No filing is needed to use the name.
- Unregistered trade names in the same sector (PV-Genie, GD Genie-Deals) are tiny and differ in overall impression from "Genie – KI-Einkaufsberater von Linker"; no action needed.

### 2.5 Not verified
- DPMAregister and EUIPO eSearch plus were not used as search interfaces (form/JS sessions); register coverage rests on TMview + tmdb.eu. Two EUIPO figurative records (014961064, 018250135) did not render.
- Unregistered rights (company and trade names) cannot be searched exhaustively; found by web search only.
- Whether AA, GENIE GmbH or Genie Holding actually use their marks in the EU for the relevant services was not established.
- Figurative marks with "Genie" only in the image may be missed; applications from the last ~2 weeks may not be indexed yet.
- UK register (192 TMview word records in 9/35/42) not analysed — UK is not the target market.
- No attorney clearance opinion was obtained; it would only become relevant if Linker wanted to register a mark.

Pattern: a persona name never ships alone — always house brand + function ("AI energy companion", "AI assistant"); the largest retailer moved from persona to umbrella name.

**EU AI Act Art. 50(1)** (https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/): providers must design AI systems intended to interact directly with natural persons so that those persons are informed they are interacting with an AI system, unless that is obvious to a reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect person. Commission FAQ (last update 2026-07-24, https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act): applies from 2026-08-02 (grace period only for Art. 50(2) marking, until 2026-12-02, for systems already on the market); inform from the start of the first interaction, clearly, distinguishably and accessibly; the "obvious" exception is to be read restrictively; covers consumers, professionals and other users. Linker develops and runs the assistant under its own name, so it is the provider (Art. 3(3)) and deployer. "Genie" is a persona name and does not itself disclose AI -> the label and first message must say "AI"/"KI". The Digital Omnibus did not move Art. 50(1) (FAQ above; https://www.activemind.legal/de/guides/ki-transparenzpflichten-marketing/ , 2026-07-21).
