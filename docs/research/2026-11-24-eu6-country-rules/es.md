# Spain · generation access and connection permits for self-consumption

The consolidated Spanish original of RD 244/2019 was read on 24 November 2026. BOE gives 7 April 2019 for entry into force of the decree and 21 March 2026 for its most recent consolidated update. Article 7.1(b)(i–iii) exempts the generating installation in the no-surplus self-consumption mode. In the surplus mode, the exemption requires both installed capacity at or below 15 kW and urbanised land equipped with the services required by planning law. The remaining surplus cases require generation access and connection permits. Article 7.1(a) treats the consumer connection separately. [BOE, RD 244/2019, Art. 7](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2019/04/05/244/con#a7)

For PV, Article 3(h) uses maximum inverter output, summed over the inverters when applicable. Article 4.2(a) uses 100 kW as one of several conditions for simplified surplus compensation. That threshold does not determine the exemption under Article 7. Unknown export mode, statutory installed power or land status remains open for the installer and operator.
