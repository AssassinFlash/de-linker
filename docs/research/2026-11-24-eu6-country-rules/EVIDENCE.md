## Italy

CEI's October 2025 consolidated working edition includes V3. Section 8.6.2, printed pp. 97–98, distinguishes integrated converter protection or a dedicated relay up to and including 11.08 kW, and a dedicated relay above 11.08 kW. Use the whole plant's generating power at an LV distribution connection. An LV terminal on equipment at a site connected at medium voltage does not establish this scope. The rule does not determine equipment conformity. Connection applications follow the operator's applicable TICA route. The consolidated cover does not establish the original effective date of this SPI provision. The snapshot therefore supports the recorded reading date. ## Poland

The Sejm's consolidated Renewable Energy Sources Act, dated 3 August 2026 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 68 as amended), defines the renewable-electric microinstallation at Art. 2(19), PDF page 9. This reading covers the electrical network branch with installed electrical power at most 50 kW and nominal connection voltage strictly below 110 kV. The alternative combined-heat provision is outside this electrical planning input. Classification does not establish entitlement to connect through notification; the operator must check connection power, procedure and evidence. Date applicability is confined to the observed snapshot. [Polish official consolidated text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/68/text/U/D20260068Lj.pdf#page=9) · [PTPiREE published equipment certificates](https://ptpiree.pl/kodeksy-sieci/wykaz-certyfikatow/) · [Text extract](pl-excerpts.txt)

## Belgium

Synergrid C10/11, French edition 2.4 of 15 October 2025, §4.1.7, Table 2 and note 9, pp. 19–20. At LV, read the two totals separately: maximum AC apparent power of generation including hybrids and of separate storage. The standard limits are 5 kVA per group for single-phase connections and 10 kVA per group for three-phase connections. Footnote 9 allows a higher single-phase limit when published by the relevant DSO. Meeting these numeric limits alone does not establish a small installation or permission: the other §4.1.7 conditions, approved equipment, regional procedure and operator's requirements still apply. The separate 2026 consultation text was not used as an effective amendment. The snapshot uses the French original.
