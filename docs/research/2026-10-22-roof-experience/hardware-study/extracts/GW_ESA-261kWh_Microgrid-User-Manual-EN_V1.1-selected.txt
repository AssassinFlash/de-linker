2.1.1 STS Solution

NOTICE


1. The energy storage system that is directly connected to the STS for
communication must be less than or equal to 30 m away from the STS.
2. The communication cable length between the STS and the nearest energy
storage system should not exceed 45 m, and the communication cable length
between two adjacent energy storage systems should not exceed 5 m.
3. It is recommended to install a UPS between the STS and SEC3000C. With the UPS
installed, the system can implement remote black start; without the UPS, remote
black start cannot be implemented.
4. For STS port descriptions, refer to the user manual of the corresponding STS.
