# Usage Conditions & Data Protection

## 1\. Licence & usage conditions

The information provided by PVGIS S is free and there are no restrictions on its use.  

## 2\. Data Protection

For information about data protection policies, please see:

*   the [European Commission's privacy policy](https://ec.europa.eu/info/privacy-policy_en)
*   note on the JRC operational environment in the [Register of the Data Protection Officer](https://ec.europa.eu/dpo-register/detail/DPO-2067)
