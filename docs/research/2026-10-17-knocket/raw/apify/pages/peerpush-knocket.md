## About Knocket

Knocket is a 100% free contact layer for indie developers and small teams looking for their first 100 users. One line of code adds a live chat widget to your website. A shareable contact page (Linktree-style with socials, booking links, and blog) can replace your Contact form entirely. Every message from Live Chat, Telegram, and Email lands in one unified inbox — and you can reply directly from Telegram without opening any dashboard. What's included, free forever: - Live chat widget for websites (WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Wix, Ghost, Shopify, Next.js) - Mobile Widget for iOS / Android / Flutter / React Native (via WebView) - Shareable contact page — no site required - Unified inbox: Live Chat, Telegram, Email - Reply from Telegram — no dashboard needed - Meeting scheduler, multi-language, light/dark themes - Companion open-source AI auto-reply agent 100% free forever. No ads. No seat limits. No paid tier. Powered by Tencent RTC for global low-latency delivery.
