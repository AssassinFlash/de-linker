## Frequently asked questions

How does install work?

Paste one `<script>` tag before `</body>` on any page. The widget loads async, appears as a chat bubble in the corner. Copy the tag from the **Installation** page in your console — it already carries the `identifier` that points at your Knocket account.

Do visitors need to sign up to chat?

No sign-up. They tap the bubble and start typing. When you're offline, an email/phone form appears instead — same thread continues when you're back.

What can I customize?

From the Knocket console: **colors, welcome message, online-status style, offline form fields**. Changes apply live — no redeploy.

Can users send images or files?

Yes. **Images, videos, and files** — everything comes through as attachments in your inbox and Telegram.

Read receipts and message recall?

Two-way **read receipts** — you see when the visitor read your reply. Message **recall** works both ways — mistyped a reply? Recall it before they read it.

Can I identify visitors in my inbox?

Yes. Pass a `data-user-id` attribute on the script tag, or set it after login. Every message ties to that identity across sessions.
