# Faq | My Account | How does the Zalando Assistant work?

FAQ categories

## What can the Zalando Assistant help me with?

**Fashion and Beauty:** You can ask the Zalando Assistant anything related to fashion or beauty. It can help you find the right products on Zalando that you'll love and you can purchase there directly, as well as provide advice on fashion questions and beauty products. Just let it know what you're looking for or what you need help with with a casual two-sided conversation.

**Order Status:** Additionally you can ask the Zalando Assistant about the current status of your orders placed at Zalando. Just click on the button “Where is my order” and identify the respective orders proposed by the Zalando Assistant.

## How does the Zalando Assistant work?

The Zalando Assistant leverages premier generative AI technology. While we connect directly to OpenAI and Gemini (Google) for certain capabilities, we also integrate other leading model providers (such as Anthropic) via AWS Bedrock. This dual approach allows us to host models within Zalando’s dedicated cloud infrastructure, adding an extra layer of security and data isolation for those services.

When you submit a question or an image, the information is processed by the respective AI system. Following our strict prompting instructions, the technology analyzes your request, the GenAI technology will analyse your questions or uploaded image and reformulate your input in a way that Zalando can provide fitting product recommendations based on your questions and images by calling internal APIs. The Zalando Assistant also takes into account where you are on the Zalando platform and provides tailored conversation starters accordingly. For instance, if a customer is browsing the denim section for women and opens the Zalando Assistant, it will acknowledge this with prompts like, "I see you are interested in Women's jeans" and suggest relevant queries such as "Show me flared jeans". To make your product discovery easier, Zalando Assistant recommends products that best match your previous behaviour on the Zalando platform. You can access your last chat in the assistant for 20 minutes after the chat ended. After this you can always start a new chat by clicking on the Fashion Assistant icon again.

## Can I also talk to a human to get advice?

The Zalando Assistant is not supported by a human advisor. It is meant to be a machine learning-based advisor which uses data and algorithms to provide guidance in fashion or to give you updates on your order status. In case you have other questions, please visit our self-help pages under [https://www.zalando.de/faq](https://www.zalando.de/faq) .

## If the Zalando Assistant is not human, then how does the Zalando Assistant make an assessment?

The Zalando Assistant relies on your questions and conversations, the place on the Zalando platform where you started the Zalando Assistant, your previous behaviour on the Zalando platform as well as AI partners' special abilities to provide appropriate answers and produce suitable recommendations for your needs.

## How do you ensure that answers are fair and correct?

The Zalando Assistant responds specifically to the content of your request, following Zalando's clear, neutral instructions to the AI models.It is designed to provide safe responses and to focus exclusively on topics within the fashion and beauty domain or your current order status. Additionally, a malicious intent classifier is in place to prevent the Assistant from responding to potentially unsafe questions (for example, queries about harmful activities such as building a bomb). However, please note that AI-generated answers can sometimes be subjective, inaccurate, or influenced by biases. For more information about the underlying AI models, please refer to our partners' policies, such as OpenAI at [https://openai.com/policies](https://openai.com/policies) , Google at [https://cloud.google.com/privacy](https://cloud.google.com/privacy) and Anthropic at [https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)

With regards to beauty products please take note that the AI-generated recommendations are not a substitute for professional medical consultation on skin and hair. Use at your own risk; always consult a dermatologist for skin and hair concerns.

## How can I get back to my last chat?

You can get back to your previous chat by clicking on the Zalando Assistant icon on the same device. However, it will only be available for 20 minutes after you wrote your last chat message.

## Will my chat data be shared with someone outside of Zalando?

The Zalando Assistant utilises the information you provide within the chat and shares it with our AI service providers (such as OpenAI, Google and Anthropic) in order to generate the most suitable answers to your questions. However, these providers will not have access to your Zalando customer account data and will not use your chat to train their models.

## What should I keep in mind when uploading images?

By uploading the imagery, you grant Zalando and its integrated AI providers the necessary rights and licenses to make use of the offered services and confirm you hold all image and privacy rights. You accept our [Community Guidelines](https://www.zalando.de/faq/community-guidelines.html) and acknowledge our [Privacy Policy](https://www.zalando.de/zalando-privacy-policy/) .

## Can the Assistant give me medical advice for skin or hair conditions?

No. The Zalando Assistant is a shopping guide, not a medical professional. Our AI-generated beauty recommendations are meant to help you discover products and should be used as a starting point, not as a substitute for professional medical consultation. Please use these suggestions at your own risk, always consult a dermatologist for specific skin or hair concerns, and refrain from sharing sensitive health data in the chat.

## Who can I contact in case I experience problems?

If you experience any problems, please contact our customer service team.
