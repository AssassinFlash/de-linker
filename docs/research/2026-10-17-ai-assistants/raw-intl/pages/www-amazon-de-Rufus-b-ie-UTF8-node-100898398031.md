# Amazon.de: : All Departments

What is Rufus?

Rufus is an AI-powered shopping experience that lets you ask all kinds of shopping questions on the Amazon Shopping app on your smartphone and on Amazon.co.ukon your desktop. Some ideas to get you started:

*   Get help with something you’re not yet an expert on: “What do I need for a weekend camping trip?”
*   Find out more about a topic that interests you: “What are different types of coffee makers?”
*   Get product info that matters to you: “What’s the battery life of this laptop?”
*   Check the status of an order: "Where's my shampoo order?"  
    

  
How do I get started with Rufus?  

On your smartphone:

1.  Go to your smartphone’s app store to make sure your Amazon Shopping app is up to date.
2.  Once it’s updated, force restart the app.
3.  Open the app and tap on the Rufus button on the navigation bar in the bottom right corner of the screen. If you don’t see it, try force restarting the app again.
4.  The Rufus chat window will appear. Type or tap on the microphone to ask your question.
5.  Rufus will answer your question in the chat window. You can tap on suggested questions or ask follow-up questions.
6.  When you’re done, you can swipe down on the chat window or tap on the X button, and go back to searching the way you’ve always done it in the top search bar.  
    

  
On your desktop or laptop:

1.  Open a web browser and go to Amazon.co.uk
2.  Go to the top left side of the navigation bar. You’ll see the Rufus button.
3.  Click the Rufus button and the Rufus chat window will appear on the bottom left corner of the page.
4.  With the Rufus chat window open, you can ask a question or choose one of the suggested questions.
5.  When you’re done, you can click the X button to close the chat window or the minimise button to drop it down the the lower left hand corner of the page. From there, you can go back to searching the way you’ve always done it in the top search bar.  
    

  
Heads up: Right now, Rufus works on the Amazon Shopping app for all UK customers. Rufus on desktop is currently available to a subset of customers, and will soon become available to all customers in the United Kingdom.  

How does Rufus come up with answers to my questions?

Rufus uses information from a variety of sources to provide answers to your shopping questions, including information from Amazon's product catalogue, customer reviews, community Q&As, licensed content partners, and information from across the web. You can always find more information about a product on the detail page. Since this experience is designed for shopping, please don’t rely on it for professional, medical, legal, or financial advice. Rufus may personalize its responses to you, including based on your preferences, interests, and other information derived from your shopping activity or your conversations with Rufus.  

Where can I view and manage my saved preferences, interests, and other information about me?

To easily view and manage your preferences, interests, and information about you, simply ask Rufus (e.g. ask “what do you know about me”).

How can I provide feedback?

With this experience, you can use everyday language and ask follow-up questions when you search. Since this is an AI-powered experience, it may not always get things right. Please let us know how it’s going by giving a thumbs up or thumbs down. Need help? Our Customer Service team is always here.  

Why did I get a response of ‘I can’t answer that’ to my question?

Some responses may not be available right now, but we are actively working to answer more questions. We may also choose not to respond to certain questions.  

Can I access and clear my chat history?

Yes, here’s how to clear your chat history on the Amazon Shopping app:

1.  Tap on the Rufus button.
2.  Tap on the three-dot menu in the upper right corner.
3.  Tap Manage chat.
4.  Tap Clear chat history.

We use your chats with Rufus to provide context for future responses for up to 28 days or until you clear your chat history from view. Clearing your chat history removes all previous questions and responses from your view. Your cleared history won’t be used as context for future responses.  

Can I still use search as I always have?

Yes. You can still search by typing a product name or keywords in the search bar. The experience you are testing is an addition to the current search functionality. Rufus and search work together to answer shopping questions and find relevant products.  

What data is collected and how is it used?

We collect and use personal information in accordance with our Privacy Notice. If you do not want Amazon to use certain information, like sensitive personal data, for the purposes described in the [Amazon.co.uk Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?ie=UTF8&nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ&ref_=cct_cg_RUFUSUK_2a1&pf_rd_p=6cdfe0f6-d92f-423b-986d-365138bd7ef7&pf_rd_r=WQAR4VRBWAVGMC1E54YS), do not share it with Rufus.

Can Amazon Business customers use Rufus?

Rufus is currently only available to Amazon Business customers through the Amazon Shopping app. If you are signed into your Amazon Business account, follow the instructions on how to get started with Rufus on your smartphone. To use Rufus on your desktop or laptop, sign into a different Amazon account that is not associated with an Amazon Business membership. Rufus is currently not available on the Amazon Business app.
