# Amazon.com: : All Departments

## FAQs

## How can Alexa help me shop in the Amazon Store?

You can ask Alexa all kinds of shopping questions in the Amazon Shopping app, and on [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/?_encoding=UTF8&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67) on your desktop and mobile website. Some ideas to get you started:

*   Get help with something you're not yet an expert on: "What do I need for a weekend camping trip?"
*   Find out more about a topic that interests you: "What are different types of coffee makers?"
*   Get product info that matters to you: "What's the battery life of this laptop?"
*   Check the status of an order: "Where's my shampoo order?"

## How do I start chatting?

Alexa for Shopping is available to US customers in the Amazon Shopping app, and on Amazon.com on your desktop and mobile website. To access Alexa while in the Amazon store, simply update your Amazon Shopping app and tap the Alexa icon in the navigation bar. On Amazon.com, desktop users can find the Alexa for Shopping icon at the top left of their screen, and mobile website users can find it in the top right corner of the screen, next to the search bar.

## How is this different from Rufus?

Amazon is bringing Rufus and Alexa together to create one AI assistant with shared memory. In the Amazon store, this appears as Alexa for Shopping.

## Are preferences and information that I share in the Alexa app or on Alexa-enabled devices applied to my chats in the Amazon store?

Alexa maintains context for the profile you’re logged into and remembers conversations across the Amazon Shopping app, Amazon.com desktop and mobile website, as well as Alexa on Echo devices, Alexa.com, the Alexa app, Fire tablets, and Fire TV, so you can easily pick up where you left off. Each Alexa experience is tailored to how you engage on different devices, ensuring you get the most relevant assistance in the moment, wherever you are.

To ensure your memories and preferences are reflected across every Alexa experience, you’ll need to ensure your Alexa is connected to the Amazon account you use for shopping and then select your shopping profile.

When you first use Alexa for Shopping, you can select your shopping profile. You can also select it manually at any time.

In the Amazon Shopping app, under your account details, you can select a profile from the dropdown menu; on Amazon.com, hover over “Accounts & Lists” and click “Who’s Shopping? Select a profile.”

## Where can I view and manage my saved preferences, interests, and other information about me?

To easily view and manage your preferences, interests, and information about you, go to the About You page. To access the About You page, tap on your account or name in the Amazon Shopping app or Amazon.com. Then click on the About you link.

## Can I access my shopping chat history?

In the Amazon Shopping app, and Amazon.com desktop and mobile website, you can view your recent chats that took place with Alexa within the store. You can review and manage your chats with Alexa across devices on the [Review Alexa History page](https://www.amazon.com/alexa-privacy/apd/rvh?_encoding=UTF8&ref=1&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67).

## Where can I review and manage my chat history with Alexa?

Alexa is designed to protect your privacy. You can review and manage your Alexa interactions — including your shopping conversations with Alexa — on the [Review Alexa History page](https://www.amazon.com/alexa-privacy/apd/rvh?_encoding=UTF8&ref=1&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67).

## Which Alexa capabilities are available in the Amazon Shopping app and Amazon.com?

This experience is designed to help you shop. Not all Alexa functionality is available in the Amazon store. For example, if you ask Alexa to set a timer or turn on your living room lights while in the store, you’ll be redirected to another Alexa experience (such as Alexa.com or the Alexa app) to help carry out your request.

## Can I use Alexa for Shopping if I don’t have an Alexa+ subscription?

Alexa is available to all Amazon customers through the Amazon Shopping app and on Amazon.com, regardless of whether or not you have Prime, own an Alexa-enabled device, or have the Alexa app installed on your phone. Simply sign in to your Amazon account to start shopping with Alexa.

## Can I use my voice to shop with Alexa?

In the Amazon Shopping app, you can tap on the mic icon in the Alexa for Shopping chat window to use your voice to chat with Alexa. The audio recordings of your voice requests in the Amazon Shopping app are automatically deleted, but we retain the transcripts of those requests, as well as any requests you type to Alexa, until you choose to delete them. Learn more about voice shopping in the [Amazon Shopping app](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?ie=UTF8&nodeId=THQ1gV8HWJ6jzUS6ed&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67).

## How does Alexa come up with answers to my shopping questions?

Alexa uses information from a variety of sources to provide answers to your shopping questions, including information from Amazon's product catalog, customer reviews, community Q&As, licensed content partners, and information from across the web. You can always find more information about a product on the product detail page. Alexa may personalize its responses to you, including based on your preferences, interests, and other information derived from your shopping activity or your conversations with Alexa.

## How can I provide feedback?

Alexa may not always get it right. We are constantly auditing AI-generated responses to improve quality. Please let us know how it's going by giving a thumbs up or thumbs down.

## Can Amazon Business customers shop with Alexa?

Amazon Business customers can shop with Alexa on [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/?_encoding=UTF8&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67) by signing in through their Amazon Business account. Alexa for Shopping is currently not available on the Amazon Business mobile app.

## What are the price history graphs that are displayed in the chat?

We strive to provide you with price information that helps you make informed purchase decisions. We may show an item's price history, for example looking back 30, 90, or 365 days, to show the lowest Featured Offer price for each day or week and any shipping fees added by sellers. The Featured Offer price is the offer that appears with the Add to cart or Buy Now buttons. Please note that while we strive for accuracy, price history graphs are provided for reference and may not capture every price change.

## How can I create and manage price alerts with Alexa?

You can create price alerts via Alexa for Shopping in the Amazon store, Alexa+ on an Alexa-enabled device, the Alexa app, or Alexa.com. You can also view and manage those alerts from any of these locations. If you create price alerts via the Amazon Shopping app, you will get notifications in the Amazon Shopping app, and Alexa+ customers will also get notifications on their Alexa-enabled devices. Alexa+ customers who create price alerts via Alexa-enabled devices will receive notifications on their devices, but will only get mobile notifications if they've previously created a price alert in the Amazon Shopping app.

## When I submit a search query in the search bar in the Amazon Shopping app, why do I sometimes get routed to Alexa for Shopping?

When you submit a search query in the Amazon Shopping app, if we determine that your search query is best answered by Alexa, we may take you to Alexa for Shopping and start a conversation so you can easily ask follow-up questions. If you want to stop chatting with Alexa and get standard product search results instead, you can always do that as well.

## When does Alexa provide proactive content to help me on my shopping journey?

As you navigate [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/?_encoding=UTF8&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67) or the Amazon Shopping app, Alexa may proactively surface tips, insights, and other content to help you as you shop. For example, when you search for marathon running shoes and receive product search results, you may also receive proactive insights from Alexa about different types of marathon running shoes, tips on running techniques, or other content relevant to your product search query. Alexa may also suggest refinements to your product search query, which when tapped, updates your product search results. To start a chat with Alexa, you can tap on a suggested question or ask a question in the Alexa for Shopping chat window. The tips, insights, and other content that Alexa provides proactively will not appear in your Alexa History, but any requests you submit to Alexa through the Alexa for Shopping chat window will appear there so you can review and manage them. See the [Alexa and Alexa Device FAQ](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?ie=UTF8&nodeId=201602230&ref_=cct_cg_RufGSM_1a1&pf_rd_p=02879299-4085-43f6-8264-79b87e83cba7&pf_rd_r=6X96VFN9VG9WR6D7TW67) “Can I review and delete my Alexa voice recordings, text transcripts, and typed requests to Alexa?” for more information about how to review and manage your requests to Alexa.

## How can I share images with Alexa in the Amazon Shopping app?

You can tap "+" in the Alexa for Shopping chat window to take or upload an image and ask Alexa questions about it to help you shop. For example, if you upload a photo of a jacket you like, Alexa can identify the style, highlight key features, and help you find similar options or compare alternatives.

## Can I review the images I share with Alexa in the Amazon Shopping app?

You can review images you’ve shared with Alexa in the Amazon Shopping app for up to 30 days by going to Settings (via the three vertical dots in the Alexa for Shopping chat window), then selecting Chat History.
