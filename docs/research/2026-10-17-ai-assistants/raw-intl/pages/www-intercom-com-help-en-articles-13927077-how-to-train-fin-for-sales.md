# Build your playbook

To get started, go to [Fin AI Agent > Sales > Train](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/automation/fin-ai-agent/sales-development).

Begin with a prompt to create your playbook, which defines how Fin qualifies leads and the next steps it can guide them toward and becomes the foundation of your qualification logic, guidance, and routing outcomes.

Alternatively, you can generate your Fin for Sales configuration by simply providing your website's pricing page URL. Fin will use the information on that page to automatically generate a configuration based on your pricing details. This helps you get started faster without having to manually define everything from scratch.

### 1\. Define your qualification criteria

Tell Fin what signals matter when deciding whether someone is qualified, using clear, objective characteristics such as:

*   Company size or segment
    
*   Industry
    
*   Core use case or intent
    
*   Budget fit
    
*   Region
    
*   Existing vs. net-new customer
    

Clear criteria make Fin's qualification decisions consistent and reliable.​

### 2\. Set the routing outcomes Fin can present

Routing outcomes are the end-states Fin can guide someone to once it has qualified (or disqualified) them.

Examples of routing outcomes:

*   **Book a call** with your sales team.
    
*   **Start a trial** or **self-serve** onboarding.
    
*   **Escalate to support.**
    
*   **Politely disqualify** if the lead doesn’t meet your criteria.
    

These outcomes determine what Fin offers and says. They **do not** handle tagging, assignments, or workflow automation - those happen later in **Workflow actions** once your playbook has been created.

### 3\. Add guidance

Describe any rules or guidance you want Fin to follow such as communication style and tone of voice, as well as critical rules on how to handle references to competitors.

### 4\. Provide a few example scenarios

Include 2–3 short examples of what a qualified and non-qualified lead looks like to help Fin generalize accurately.

**Tips:**

*   You can paste information from documents to help generate your playbook. Uploading files is not supported.
    
*   Fin for Sales can use [content already added for service](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7837514-add-your-support-content-for-fin-ai-agent) in your workspace.

# Configure Fin for Sales

After you enter your prompt, click **Continue** and Fin will generate a playbook (this can take 2 - 3 minutes).

### Data to collect

At the top of the Playbook, you'll find a **Data to collect** section. Any attributes added in **Data to collect** are shared across all qualifying outcomes.

**Note:** Attributes added in **Data to collect** do not apply to **Escalate to Support** and **Disqualified** by default.

### Outcomes

Default outcomes always include:

*   **Disqualify** for spam or malicious intent.
    
*   **Support** for troubleshooting or complex support queries.
    

Additional outcomes will be created based on your prompt (e.g., self-serve, sales handover) and you can add more outcomes at the bottom of the page. Each outcome has an **Outcome type** toggle — set it to **Sales** for prospects who should be routed to your sales team (e.g., to book a call), or **Self-serve** for prospects who are happy to start a trial or sign up on their own. Sales and self-serve outcomes are counted and billed differently. See [Fin AI Agent outcomes](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205718-fin-ai-agent-outcomes) for pricing details.

<signed-url-removed>

Each routing outcome has several configuration options:

*   **[Conditions to qualify](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/13927077-how-to-train-fin-for-sales#h_ebafcccfc4)** - Define when a lead should match this outcome. Fin will ask relevant questions to determine whether a lead meets your criteria.
    
*   **[Additional data to collect](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/13927077-how-to-train-fin-for-sales#h_72715af31c)** - Choose any extra information Fin should gather before routing a lead to this outcome.
    
*   **[Actions](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/13927077-how-to-train-fin-for-sales#h_962a012688) -** Choose what happens when a lead is routed to this outcome.
    
*   **Outcome type**: The two are counted and charged differently.
    
    *   Choose **Sales** (for meeting-booking and sales follow-up) or
        
    *   **Self-serve** (for prospects directed to sign up or start a trial). Self-serve outcomes hide meeting booking and limit follow-up workflows to tagging and branching. Booking provider links can't be saved in a self-serve outcome's instruction text.

### Conditions to qualify

In the **Conditions to qualify** section, you can define two types of conditions:

*   **Prerequisite conditions** that must be met before routing happens.
    
*   **Optional conditions**, where at least one must be met for the lead to qualify.
    

A lead will qualify only after all prerequisite conditions are met and at least one optional condition is satisfied (if provided).

This also allows you to break down complex logic into smaller, more meaningful conditions. Combined with condition titles, it makes organization and readability much smoother.

<signed-url-removed>

**Tip:** You can give a title to each condition. This is purely for organization and readability. It helps you quickly understand what each condition represents. Titles do not affect routing logic.

If you see "Untitled Condition" when loading an existing config, that's expected. The old UI did not support condition titles. Once you add titles in the new UI, they will persist.

### Additional data to collect

Each outcome can still collect its own specific data. Inside every outcome's side drawer, you'll see a section called **Additional data to collect**.

Attributes added here apply only to that specific route and do not affect others. This gives you flexibility to collect general data that applies to most outcomes, while still capturing route-specific information when needed.

<signed-url-removed>

### When faced with support queries

Inside the **Escalate to support** outcome, you'll find a **When faced with support queries** section. Toggle **Hand off to Fin for Service if available** to control whether Fin passes the conversation to Fin for Service when a support query is identified.

<signed-url-removed>

When enabled, full conversation context is passed to Fin for Service. If Fin for Service can't resolve the issue, the conversation continues down the **If Fin is unable to qualify** path on your deployment workflow. This setting requires Fin for Service to be deployed.

### Actions

Choose what happens when a lead is routed to this outcome, including what Fin should communicate and taking actions to book a meeting (via the Calendly integration) or use a follow-up workflow.

<signed-url-removed>

Click **Add workflow** if you want follow-up steps beyond Fin’s final message. Examples of actions after being qualified include:

*   Assign to sales team inbox.
    
*   Book a meeting via Calendly or Chili Piper Concierge.
    
*   Tag conversation.
    

**Note:**

*   When a lead reaches an outcome, the conversation closes automatically if it stays inactive, unless it's already been assigned to a teammate or team. This only works when you have the bot inbox enabled in [Settings > AI & Automation > Automation](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/ai-automation/automation?section=operator-inbox).
    
*   The inactivity timer used depends on your [lead recovery settings](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/13927077-how-to-train-fin-for-sales#h_dfb7926911): if lead recovery is enabled with a timer configured, that timer applies. If lead recovery is off or no timer is set, the [global inactivity timer](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/ai-automation/automation?section=operator-inbox) is used instead.
    

## Lead recovery

Use the Lead recovery section of your playbook to define what happens when a prospect stops responding before qualification is complete.

Choose one of three actions:

*   **Let Fin send a personalized follow-up:** Fin sends a recovery message to re-engage the prospect. The message is sent via the original channel, and also over email if the prospect's email address was captured during the conversation.
    
*   **Trigger a workflow:** hand off to a custom workflow for more complex recovery logic or to hand to a human teammate.
    
*   **Close the conversation:** automatically close the conversation with no further outreach.
    

<signed-url-removed>

Set an inactivity timer to control how long Fin waits before triggering the recovery action. If the prospect replies to a recovery message, Fin resumes qualification with full context preserved.

## Guidance

Guidance is where you shape how Fin sounds and how it handles the moments that come up again and again in your sales conversations. Your playbook decides where a prospect ends up. Guidance decides how Fin gets them there.

Go to [Fin AI Agent > Sales > Train > Guidance](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/automation/fin-ai-agent/sales/guidance).

**Note:**

*   Guidance text is generated from your prompt and can be edited.
    
*   Existing [guidance](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/10210126-provide-fin-ai-agent-with-specific-guidance) you've configured for Service does not apply to Fin for Sales.
    
*   Guidance works differently in Fin for Sales than in Fin for Service. In Service, guidance can influence when Fin escalates a conversation. In Sales it cannot. Where a prospect goes is decided entirely by your playbook.
    

## What Fin already does without guidance

Fin for Sales has a set of behaviors built in. You don't need to write guidance for any of these, and writing them uses space you could give to something specific to your business:

*   Keeps replies short and conversational rather than formal or robotic
    
*   Tries to understand the prospect's problem before pitching
    
*   Doesn't raise pricing unless the prospect asks, and quotes prices only from your content
    
*   Never recommends a competitor
    
*   Only shares links that come from your playbook, your content, or your Guidance
    
*   Doesn't ask again for something the prospect has already told it
    
*   Wraps up quickly when a prospect seems in a hurry
    

The most common mistake we see is guidance that repeats one of these. It costs you nothing, but it gains you nothing either.

**Tip:** If a piece of guidance contains no product name, no number, and no judgement call that another company would make differently, it's probably already default behavior.

## What Guidance can and can't affect

Guidance shapes what Fin says. It doesn't change what Fin can do.

**Guidance can:**

*   Change the words Fin uses
    
*   Change when it asks for contact details, and how many things it asks for at once
    
*   Tell Fin how to respond in a specific situation
    
*   Give Fin facts about your business that aren't in your content
    
*   Give Fin a URL it's allowed to share
    

**Guidance can't:**

*   Route a prospect, or change the conditions for a routing outcome
    
*   Hand a conversation to your team
    
*   Book a meeting, create a CRM record, or trigger a workflow
    
*   Change which attributes Fin must collect before routing
    
*   Quote a price that isn't in your content
    
*   Make Fin aware of time — it doesn't know whether it's within business hours, and can't set a wait timer.
    

Those are configured in your playbook, your outcomes, and your integrations. If you write guidance asking Fin to do one of them, Fin may try, but it will either fail or tell the prospect something that isn't going to happen.

## Communication style

Use this for the things Fin can't guess: your product names, the terms your company uses, spelling conventions, and anything you'd rather it never said.

**Examples:**

*   Always write in British English, including spelling and date formats.
    
*   Call it a walkthrough, not a demo.
    
*   Write the product name in full: "Acme Insights", not "Insights".
    
*   Keep it formal. No contractions.
    

## Conversation strategy & flow

Use this to set when Fin asks for contact details, and whether it asks for one thing at a time or several together. Guidance doesn't affect routing. Which outcomes exist, their conditions, and attributes Fin must collect before routing are all configured in your playbook.

**Examples:**

*   Ask for an email address before answering their first question.
    
*   Ask for everything you need in one message rather than one at a time.
    

## Situational responses

Use this for the situations that come up often and have a right answer. Write each one as a rule and say what Fin should do instead: "Never do X. If a prospect asks, do Y." Keep each rule to a single situation. This changes what Fin says, not what Fin does. Guidance doesn't hand a conversation over to your team; escalation is configured in your playbook.

**Examples:**

*   Never compare us feature-by-feature to a competitor. If a prospect asks, ask what isn't working for them today instead.
    
*   Never tell a prospect whether they qualify for our startup program. Confirm the program exists and nothing more.
    

## Business context

Use this for the pages Fin is allowed to link to, and facts about your company that aren't in your content. Keep product facts and pricing in your content sources: Fin searches those before making any product claim.

**Examples:**

*   Our self-serve signup page is example.com/signup.
    
*   For technical questions, link to example.com/docs.
    

## Integrations

From [Fin AI Agent > Sales > Train > Integrations](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/automation/fin-ai-agent/sales/integrations), you can allow Fin to enrich leads using additional context and data, create leads in your existing CRM, and connect to calendars for booking meetings.

### Lead enrichment

To hold highly personalized, context-aware conversations, Fin can independently research leads and pull data from third-party enrichment platforms—all in real-time. This happens automatically after Fin captures a prospect's email address, reducing the number of follow-up questions Fin needs to ask.

### Using online research

After Fin collects a prospect's email, it'll research them online and enrich the lead with the appropriate qualification data. **Online research** is on by default, and can be toggled off at any time.

<signed-url-removed>

### CRM and data enrichment services

Use data connectors to enrich Fin's knowledge about leads with information from your existing tools, such as SFDC, HubSpot, Clay, and more.

<signed-url-removed>

[Learn how to qualify leads faster using online research and third-party enrichment tools](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/14079086-enriching-leads-with-fin-sales-agent).

### Create leads in your CRM

Automatically create or update leads in your CRM after qualification. First, you’ll need to authenticate your chosen CRM, then trigger a data connector using the follow-up workflow once a lead is qualified.

<signed-url-removed>

[Learn how to connect Fin to Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio and more](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/13128480-use-crm-data-connectors-with-fin-sales-agent).

**Tip:** If you're using Salesforce, you can skip the manual setup. Fin for Sales can connect directly to your Salesforce account and build a working data connector for you in minutes — no API knowledge, code, or manual field mapping required. [Learn more in our guide to Connect Salesforce to Fin for Sales](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/15703879-connect-salesforce-to-fin-for-sales).

### Calendars for booking meetings

By connecting to [Calendly](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/15733346-book-meetings-with-calendly-using-fin-for-sales) or [Chili Piper Concierge](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/15449655-book-meetings-with-chili-piper-using-fin-for-sales), you can let qualified leads book time with your team directly in Messenger, or via a scheduling link in email and SMS. See [Fin AI Agent > Sales > Train > Integrations](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/automation/fin-ai-agent/sales/integrations) to connect a calendar.

<signed-url-removed>

**Note:**

*   Fin will fetch availability for the next two weeks from [Calendly](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/15733346-book-meetings-with-calendly-using-fin-for-sales) (you must be on a paid Calendly plan).
    
*   Fin will fetch availability for the next 60 days from [Chili Piper](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/15449655-book-meetings-with-chili-piper-using-fin-for-sales).

# Test Fin for Sales

To test Fin for Sales, go to [Fin AI Agent > Sales](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/automation/fin-ai-agent/sales-development) and click **Test** in the top right to use the preview panel and validate Fin's logic and messaging before deployment.

You can:

*   Run sample conversations to see how routing decisions are made.
    
*   Validate required attributes such as email, company size, and problem statement.
    
*   Confirm routing logic for each outcome.
    
*   Review message quality Fin's behavior.
