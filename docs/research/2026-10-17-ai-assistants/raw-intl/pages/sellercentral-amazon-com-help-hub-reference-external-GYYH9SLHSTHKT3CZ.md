# Alexa for shopping

Designed to help you shop, the **Alexa for Shopping** experience is available to US customers in the Amazon Shopping app and on Amazon.com. To access Alexa while in the Amazon store, update your Amazon Shopping app and tap the Alexa icon in the navigation bar. Desktop users can find the Alexa icon at the top of their screen. Customers do not need a Prime membership or an Alexa-enabled device to use Alexa for Shopping.

## How customers use Alexa for shopping

Amazon brought Rufus and Alexa together to create one AI assistant that delivers a more personal, helpful shopping experience across a wide range of devices. In the Amazon store, this appears as Alexa for Shopping.

Alexa for Shopping helps customers make informed shopping decisions. Customers can use the AI assistant for the following:

*   Get product recommendations
*   Compare options
*   Get the latest product updates
*   Review historical product pricing
*   Set price alerts and Auto Buy
*   Understand product details and read what other customers say
*   Access current and past orders
*   Answer questions that aren't obviously related to shopping

Not all Alexa functionality is available in the Amazon store. For example, if customers ask Alexa to set a timer or turn on their living room lights with Alexa for shopping, they may be redirected to another Alexa experience (such as [Alexa.com](http://alexa.com/) or the Alexa app) to help them carry out their request.

## Price history feature

Alexa for Shopping may show an item's 30-day, 90-day, or 365-day price history, which is the lowest Featured Offer price for each day or week including any shipping fees added by sellers and excluding temporary price errors. The Featured Offer price is the offer that appears with the Add to cart or Buy Now buttons.

## Provide feedback

Alexa for Shopping may not always get it right. You can provide immediate feedback by selecting thumbs up or thumbs down under any response, or by using the help center. Your feedback is important and will help us improve the experience.

For specific questions about your product such as "What are the dimensions?" or "Is this machine washable?", Alexa for Shopping uses information from the product's detail page. If you notice Alexa for Shopping answers are related to incorrect product attributes, take the following steps:  

1.  Verify that the product detail page contains the most current information
2.  Submit a ticket via the help center with:
    
    *   The product **ASIN**
    *   A screenshot of the question and Alexa's response
    *   The nature of the issue
    *   Additional context, including your expected response
    *   Supporting materials for verification:
        
        *   Screenshot of the relevant product listing section
        *   Product or user manuals containing the information
        *   Details from the manufacturer's website
