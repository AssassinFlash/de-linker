# What Do I Need to Place Orders with Alexa?

Before placing orders with Alexa, make sure that you meet these requirements.

**Note:** Visit [About Alexa for shopping](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=Tvh55TTsQ5XQSFc7Pr) for more information about shopping with Alexa on the Amazon website and mobile app.

Alexa uses the default shipping address and payment settings in Your Account.

To place orders, you need:

*   a valid payment method.
*   an Amazon account.
*   a device with access to Alexa.

**Tip:** Verify your purchasing settings in the Alexa app.
