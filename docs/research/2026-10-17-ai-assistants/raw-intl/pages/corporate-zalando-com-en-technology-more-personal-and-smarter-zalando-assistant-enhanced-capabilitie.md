Since October 2024, the [Zalando Assistant](https://en.zalando.de/campaigns/zfa/?_rfl=de) has been live for logged-in customers in all 25 Zalando markets, giving fashion advice in local languages. So far, over 2 million customers have used it to get inspired and find items they love. Now, Zalando is rolling out a significant update to its assistant, enhancing its capabilities with a vibrant new look, deeper personalisation, and increased context awareness to offer even more relevant and seamless fashion recommendations.

The new version of the assistant is now live to all logged-in customers and can be accessed via web and app with a:

## New look & feel

The refreshed look makes interactions even more engaging and seamlessly blends style with functionality. Whether customers are searching for outfit inspiration or specific products, the assistant’s enhanced visual appeal ensures an improved and enjoyable user experience. The more colourful and dynamic design of the updated assistant aligns with [Zalando’s updated brand positioning](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/fashion/zalando-unveils-new-brand-positioning-focus-quality-lifestyle-and-inspiration-updates-brand "Zalando unveils new brand positioning with focus on quality, lifestyle, and inspiration; updates brand identity").

## Deeper personalisation

The assistant is now connected to customer accounts, allowing it to understand individual preferences and shopping history. This deeper level of personalisation enables more relevant and tailored recommendations. During a pilot phase, we observed a +40% increase in high-value interactions - e.g. a like or an add to card - demonstrating how relevant and personalised suggestions enhance the shopping journey and drive customer satisfaction.

## Context awareness

Another key advancement is context awareness. The assistant can now recognise where customers are within the Zalando platform and provide tailored conversation starters accordingly. For instance, if a customer is browsing the denim section and opens the assistant, it will acknowledge this with prompts like, "I see you are interested in Women's jeans" and suggest relevant queries such as "Show me flared jeans." This level of contextual intelligence makes it easier for customers to find exactly what they need.

"We’re thrilled to bring this next evolution of our AI assistant to our customers," **says Tian Su, VP Personalisation & Recommendation at Zalando**. “The assistant is now smarter and more personalised than ever. By providing this more personalised and contextually relevant support, we are empowering our customers to make informed choices and find exactly what they're looking for. We're continuing to create a truly seamless and enjoyable experience - reflecting our commitment to building an entertaining and immersive digital shopping journey. In addition, our assistant was also upgraded to be more visually pleasing for our customers.”
