## Supplier Verification Report

### Overview

A senior sourcing manager needed to reach out to potential suppliers for stainless steel water bottles. The goal was to draft a professional RFQ (Request for Quotation) email that clearly communicated product specifications, order quantities, and commercial terms — all within a realistic B2B context.

The manager turned to Accio AI to generate a polished supplier inquiry email, then asked for a tighter version with a clear call-to-action and reply deadline.

### Initial Draft: Detailed Supplier Inquiry

Accio AI first produced a comprehensive RFQ email of 150–200 words. The draft covered all essential sourcing details:

*   Capacity: 500 ml
*   Material: 304 food-grade stainless steel
*   Type: Double-wall vacuum insulated
*   Lid: Leak-proof screw or flip-top lid
*   Finish: Matte black with additional color options
*   Branding: One-color logo printing and laser engraving
*   Packaging: Individual retail boxes or standard export cartons

The email requested unit pricing for 500, 1,000, and 3,000 units, along with MOQ, sample cost, production lead time, shipping terms (Incoterms), payment terms, and certifications such as LFGB, FDA, or BPA-free documentation. It also asked for a product catalog, high-resolution images, and customization capability details.

### Refined Email: Concise and Action-Oriented

The manager then asked Accio AI to tighten the email to under 80 words while adding a clear call-to-action with a reply deadline. The result was a focused, high-impact RFQ:

> **Subject: RFQ – Stainless Steel Water Bottles**
> 
> Dear \[Supplier Name\],
> 
> We are sourcing \[1,000\] double-wall, 500 ml, 304 stainless steel water bottles with \[logo printing/laser engraving\]. Please quote prices for \[500\], \[1,000\], and \[3,000\] units, including MOQ, sample cost, lead time, Incoterms, payment terms, and certifications.
> 
> Please reply with your quotation and catalog by \[September 10, 2026\]. We aim to confirm a supplier by \[date\].
> 
> Best regards,  
> \[Your Name\]  
> \[Company Name\]

This refined version preserved all critical commercial requirements while cutting the word count by more than half, making it faster to read and more likely to prompt a swift supplier response.
