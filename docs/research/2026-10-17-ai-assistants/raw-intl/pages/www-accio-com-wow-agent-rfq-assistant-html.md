## What RFQ Assistant can do for you

RFQ Assistant is an inquiry-to-quotation agent: it screens high-value RFQs, produces nine-section customized quotes, and builds phased follow-up plans.

### Screen high-value RFQs

Filters high-value RFQs out of your inquiries so you focus your effort where it counts.

### Nine-section customized quotes

Produces nine-section customized quotes that present your offer in a clear structure.

### Phased follow-up plans

Builds phased follow-up plans that keep deals moving at the right pace.
