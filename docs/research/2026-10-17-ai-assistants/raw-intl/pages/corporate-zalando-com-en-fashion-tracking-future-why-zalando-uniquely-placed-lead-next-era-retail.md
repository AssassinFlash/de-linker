# Zalando: Tracking the future: Why Zalando is uniquely placed to lead the next era of retail

### 2\. Building tailored worlds for every style

Our data shows that **76% of our active customers shop across multiple categories**. A key part of our strategy is to leverage AI technologies and our multi-specialist expertise to create bespoke environments for every category - from the most relevant fashion assortment, to tending Beauty products and high-performance Sports gear.

Some recent examples of category-specific enhancements include the new **Style It** feature within our Designer category. It lets customers combine pieces on an AI-generated avatar, preview full looks, and instantly purchase either the complete outfit or selected items. We have also started rolling out virtual try-on tools for beauty products. In Sports, we have launched **“Worlds of Sports”**, dedicated online hubs providing high-performance gear, inspiration, recommendations and advice for specific sports types like running, football, or basketball. 

These tailored experiences are not only good for customers, they allow our brand partners to shine, too. **We are evolving into a true retail enabler**, equipping brands with deep data insights and the marketing power of Zalando Marketing Services (ZMS). By providing these tools, we allow partners to better connect with their target audiences and determine their own assortment strategies, ensuring they have the autonomy to thrive within our tailored worlds.  
 

### 3\. B2B: Powering growth through ZEOS

Becoming a retail enabler also means **supporting our partners and the industry beyond our own platform**. ZEOS is our answer to the fragmentation of the European market. By opening our pan-European logistics network, we allow brands like Marks & Spencer, Pepe Jeans, and NEXT to manage their entire marketplace business from a single, unified stock pool.

The impact is clear: our partnership with NEXT contributed to i**nternational e-commerce growth** that was 14% points above initial guidance. We’re not stopping at the borders of Europe, either. By 2026, we’ll be expanding our trade capabilities to the US via Amazon, Walmart, and Target, while piloting new content services to help brands meet the highest product presentation standards.

## **The journey ahead** 

By combining our 15-year heritage of data with the bold possibilities of Agentic Commerce, we aren’t just watching the industry change—we’re the ones driving it forward. The future of fashion is personal, it’s frictionless, and it’s being built right here at Zalando.
