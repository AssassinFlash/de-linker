*   Log into your Amazon Business account and select Business Settings, then look for the orange Amazon Business Assistant icon in the lower right corner.
    
*   Amazon Business Assistant is available for all United States customers on desktop on select settings pages where it can help.
    
*   Alexa for Shopping can help you research and compare products, while Amazon Business Assistant is focused on helping you set up and get the most from your procurement features. Both are available to business customers.
    
*   Amazon Business Assistant uses Amazon Business and Help content to answer questions on a variety of features and programs available in the Amazon Business experience. Savings recommendations are typically calculated based on the average price you paid for the item over the past 12 months. For savings estimates that require purchasing a particular quantity of a suggested item, those estimates assume that a sufficient quantity of the item is available for purchase from the supplier suggested. If a sufficient quantity is not available from that supplier and you purchase units from a different supplier, your savings estimate may no longer apply. Knowledge of any custom pricing is limited and may not be reflected in savings guidance. Since this conversational experience is designed to help you utilize your Amazon Business account, please don't rely on it for professional, medical, legal, financial or tax advice. You should also avoid disclosing any personal or sensitive information.
    
*   Users can provide feedback on the Assistant's performance through the thumbs up/down feature. For support beyond the AI assistant, Amazon's Customer Service team is available to help.
