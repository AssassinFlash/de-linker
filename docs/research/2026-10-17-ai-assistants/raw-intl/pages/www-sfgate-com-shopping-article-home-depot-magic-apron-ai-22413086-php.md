# Can Home Depot’s AI actually help you find what you need in-store?

Home Depot's Magic Apron AI assistant claims it can now help shoppers find products and answer project questions inside more than 2,000 U.S. stores.

Billy Cadden

The next time you’re wandering the aisles of your local [Home Depot](https://www.homedepot.com/) store trying to find help, you won’t have to flag down an employee. You can ask the app instead.

The Home Depot [said](https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/company/home-depot-expands-magic-apron-deliver-personalized-localized-store-guidance) it has rolled out new in-store features for Magic Apron, its AI shopping assistant, across all of its more than 2,000 U.S. stores. Magic Apron can provide personalized help tailored to the location where you happen to be standing.

You’ll find Magic Apron inside the [Home Depot app’s](https://www.homedepot.com/c/mobile-app) Store Mode, or you can pull it up by scanning one of the QR codes posted on signs around the store. Questions can be typed, spoken, or asked with a photo. For instance, you take a picture of a bag of grass seed and ask what fertilizer suits your local climate. The tool can provide an answer and point you to the exact aisle and bay for things you need. It’ll also tell you whether something is in stock or at a nearby store.

The Home Depot said the bot isn’t meant to replace the humans on the floor. “Our associates remain at the center of the shopping experience, and Magic Apron gives customers another way to access guidance and support while they’re in our stores,” said Jordan Broggi, the company’s executive vice president of interconnected retail.

The company claims the tool already handles millions of questions a month and that shoppers in early testing said it helped them find things faster and feel more confident about what they were buying.

The Home Depot isn’t the only retailer bringing an AI chatbot to its store. Lowe’s previously launched a ChatGPT-powered assistant called Mylow for customers with home improvement questions. Meanwhile, Walmart and Amazon both have a customized chatbot designed to help people shop.

Magic Apron has been available on The Home Depot’s website and app for over a year, where it answers how-to questions and summarizes product reviews. Whether you’d rather ask an AI chatbot than a human in your local store is up to you.
