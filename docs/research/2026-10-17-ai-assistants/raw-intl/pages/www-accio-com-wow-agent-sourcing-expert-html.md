## Official prompt templates

The 23 official templates below are drawn from real sourcing scenarios and map directly to Accio Sourcing Expert; some come with official reference attachments. Click any template to expand the full prompt — copy it directly or take it to the workspace in one click; swap in your own product, budget, and quantity, and you're ready to go.

#### Automated Sourcing

*   I want to purchase \[X\] units of \[product name\] at a target unit price within \[X\] USD. Please automate the whole process for me: search for products, compare them by unit price and MOQ, and recommend the best option; once I've chosen, send an inquiry email to the supplier, follow up on the negotiation, and win me the lowest unit price and lowest MOQ.
    
*   I have a vague sourcing need — please automate the whole process: first help me refine the requirements, then find 10 different suppliers for comparison. Initial need: \[X\] custom insulated tumblers at a target unit price within \[X\] USD.
    
*   I want to sell gardening products cross-border but haven't decided what to sell. Please run the full process automatically: start with market analysis and give me 3-5 product directions with reasons; once I pick one, find 10 suppliers to compare.
    

#### Supplier Discovery

*   I'm looking for \[product name\] with a target price below \[X\] USD — find me some highly rated products on Alibaba
    
*   Official reference images
    
    Based on the attached image, find source factories on Alibaba making the same or similar items, and compare their production capacity and prices
    
*   Find Alibaba suppliers for \[product keyword\]. Requirements: Verified Supplier, OEM support, MOQ no more than \[quantity\]
    
*   I saw this product on Amazon \[link or description\] — check whether Alibaba has suppliers for the same item
    

#### Inquiry & Negotiation

*   Based on the attached purchase list, parse my requirements and send batch inquiries directly to matching suppliers
    
*   I want to purchase this item \[product link\] at a target unit price of \[X\] USD — initiate the inquiry on my behalf and handle the subsequent negotiation
    
*   Check my current inquiry progress; if there is none, tell me how to send my first inquiry
    
*   During negotiations, how will you help me push prices down automatically, handle supplier pushback, and proactively expand my supplier base?
    

#### Compare & Decide

*   Compare these products \[product links\] across price, MOQ, specifications, supplier performance, and more, and give me your recommendation with reasons
    
*   Find factories producing \[product name\] on Alibaba.com, compare the top three, and produce an analysis report
    
*   I've shortlisted this Alibaba supplier \[supplier name\] and want to buy their product \[product link\]. Verify them for me: background check, whether customs product descriptions match their main business, and an overall credibility assessment.
    
*   For the Alibaba.com product \[product link\] shipping to \[destination country/region\], estimate the freight cost for \[purchase quantity\]
    
*   Look up the US HS code and tariff rate for the Alibaba.com product \[product link\]
    
*   Look up the US customs clearance taxes and fees for purchasing \[quantity\] units of the Alibaba.com product \[product link\] at a unit price of \[X\] USD
    

#### Trend Discovery

*   I'm planning to open a store selling artificial flowers. How should I select products to find the ones most worth sourcing?
    
*   What's the market potential for pet slow-feeder bowls? Help me identify the best-selling styles and find reliable, high-quality suppliers.
    
*   Analyze the best-selling press-on nails on Amazon and, based on current market trends, give clear, actionable product-selection recommendations.

*   Design a brand-new dog slow-feeder bowl that solves common pain points.
