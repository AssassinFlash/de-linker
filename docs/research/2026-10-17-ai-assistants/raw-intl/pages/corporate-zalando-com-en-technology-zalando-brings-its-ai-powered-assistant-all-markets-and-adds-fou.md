*   **Zalando brings its AI-powered assistant to all markets and adds four new cities to its Trend Spotter**
*   **Zalando’s AI assistant – powered by Zalando’s own models and OpenAI’s large language models – is now available across Zalando’s 25 markets featuring local languages**
*   **Trend Spotter now includes Amsterdam, London, Warsaw and Zurich as new style destinations and is available to customers in all markets**
*   **Both initiatives supports Zalando’s commitment to offer customers an even more engaging and inspiring experience on its web and app**

**Berlin, October 1st, 2024 //** Zalando, one of Europe’s leading online destinations for fashion and lifestyle, is scaling its existing AI-powered offerings Zalando Assistant and Trend Spotter. While Trend Spotter now includes four additional cities, the beta version of the Zalando Assistant is now available to logged-in customers across our 25 markets in the respective local language. Both initiatives are in line with Zalando’s commitment to offering customers an even more engaging and inspiring experience on its web and app.

Zalando’s AI assistant, powered by Zalando’s own models and OpenAI’s large language models, now offers logged-in customers in all markets personalised fashion advice in local languages. It enables customers to navigate Zalando’s extensive assortment with intuitive queries such as “What should I wear to my dad’s 60th birthday in November in Barcelona?” The assistant understands context – such as location, weather, and occasion – to make informed recommendations.

“We know our customers want to get inspired when they come to Zalando. Therefore, the rollout of our Zalando Assistant to all markets is  a significant step in our commitment to enhancing the customer experience by making it easier for customers to discover fashion that suits their unique style and needs,” says **Tian Su, VP Personalisation and Recommendation at Zalando**. “At the same time, we’re now offering customers even more local inspiration by adding four new cities to our Trend Spotter – featuring emerging trends from 10 European fashion capitals in total.”

Following the successful implementation in six key markets, Zalando has now made its Trend Spotter available to customers in all its markets and introduced four new cities: Amsterdam, London, Warsaw and Zurich. Powered by Zalando’s own data, customers can discover emerging trends in ten different fashion capitals every week and see what is trending locally and across Europe. Each featured item comes with an explanation of why it is listed in Trend Spotter that week and whether it is related to a global or local trend.

These developments mark another step forward in Zalando’s journey to building the leading pan-European ecosystem for fashion and lifestyle e-commerce. Zalando remains committed to innovation and excellence, ensuring that every customer interaction is tailored, inspiring, and impactful – empowering customers to have fun and feel confident in their fashion choices and lifestyles.

**About Zalando**

Founded in Berlin in 2008, Zalando is the leading European technology platform for fashion and lifestyle. The Zalando group connects 62 million active customers with more than 7,000 brands across 29 markets. Our business is built on a unique AI-powered data and infrastructure platform. For our customers, our multi-app approach - comprising Zalando, ABOUT YOU, and Lounge by Zalando - delivers an inspiring, highly personalized shopping experience, serving different customers with different needs. For our partners, we are building the operating system for e-commerce.
