# Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction - Microsoft HAX Toolkit

## Best practices for designing AI user experiences

### What are the Guidelines?

The Guidelines are [evidence-based](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) best practices for designing your AI user experiences.

### How do I use the Guidelines?

1.  Learn about the 18 Guidelines in the [HAX Design Library](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/). Explore examples and [design patterns](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/design-patterns/) for implementing each Guideline.
2.  Use the Guidelines to plan your AI application. With your team, go through the [HAX Workbook](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/workbook/) to prioritize which Guidelines to apply and how to implement them.

The [Design Library](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/) helps you learn each Guideline. Explore examples and [design patterns](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/design-patterns/) for implementing them throughout the user experience: upon _initial_ interaction, _during_ interaction, when the AI system is _wrong,_ and _over time_.

[](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/)

### Where do these Guidelines come from?

We developed the Guidelines in response to AI practitioners telling us, _“AI is the most ambiguous space I’ve ever worked in, in my years of working in design … There aren’t any real rules and we don’t have a lot of tools.”_ 

The Guidelines are a synthesis of more than 20 years’ research, introduced in this award-winning [2019 CHI paper](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) (summary [here](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-design/)). Learn more about the process we used to create and validate them on the [About page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/about/).
