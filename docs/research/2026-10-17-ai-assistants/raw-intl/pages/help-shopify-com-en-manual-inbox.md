# Shopify Help Center | Shopify Inbox

Install Shopify Inbox from the Shopify App Store.

Shopify Inbox lets you connect with customers who visit your online store. You can manage conversations with your staff, or turn on an Inbox agent to respond to customers automatically.

### Inbox agent

Shopify Inbox includes an Inbox agent that you can turn on to chat with customers automatically. The Inbox agent answers customer questions about your products, shipping, returns, sizing, and store policies by using your product catalog, store policies, Knowledge Base facts and files, and published storefront content such as pages, blog posts, and product care guides. For a specific product in your store catalog, the Inbox agent can also use cited web search for qualitative reviews, feedback, ratings, or social proof. The Inbox agent can personalize product recommendations based on customer preferences, sizes, and purchase history when those details are available. Customers with an active Shop session can get personalized recommendations without signing in to your store.

The Inbox agent is an opt-in feature. If you already use Inbox, then your conversations stay with your staff until you choose to turn it on. You can configure the Inbox agent's settings, persona, and training from the [**Inbox**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-inbox) page in your Shopify admin. To review and respond to customer conversations, use the [Shopify Inbox app for desktop](https://inbox.shopify.com/), the [Shopify Inbox app for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/shopify-inbox/id1301681854), or the [Shopify Inbox app for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shopify.ping). To learn how to turn on the Inbox agent, set its persona, train it, review its performance, and understand its safety limits, refer to [Assigning your Inbox agent to conversations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/assigning-your-ai-staff-member) and the related pages in this section.

### Managing conversations with your staff

If you manage conversations with your staff, then you and your staff respond to customer messages directly. You can respond to messages, send product links, send discount codes, and send images from Shopify Inbox.

You can access Shopify Inbox from the [Shopify Inbox app for desktop](https://inbox.shopify.com/), the [Shopify Inbox app for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/shopify-inbox/id1301681854), and the [Shopify Inbox app for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shopify.ping). When customers send you messages, you can respond from your desktop or mobile device, and manage conversations across staff members.

You can customize your [chat settings and appearance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/chat-settings-and-appearance) and [configure Inbox settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/configure-inbox) such as notifications, quick replies, and availability hours.
