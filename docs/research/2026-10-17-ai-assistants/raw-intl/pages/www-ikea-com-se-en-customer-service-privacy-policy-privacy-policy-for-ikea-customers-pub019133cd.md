## 1\. Information on the processing of personal data

IKEA cares about your privacy, and we want you to feel safe when you provide us with your data. It is important for us at IKEA to be transparent about how IKEA processes your personal data and to provide you with information in a way that allows you to understand how we will process your personal data. 

In this privacy notice, which applies to all IKEA customers in Sweden, we provide you with information on how we handle your personal data when you visit our websites register as a member of the IKEA Business Network, apply for an IKEA Företagsfaktura, purchase our products or services, or in other contacts with us. 

IKEA is continuously working on privacy issues and we may therefore update this policy. The latest version of the privacy notice can be found at  [www.IKEA.se/integritetspolicy](https://www.ikea.com/se/en/customer-service/privacy-policy/privacy-policy-for-ikea-customers-pub019133cd/). 

You are always welcome to contact us if you have any questions or comments.

If you are an IKEA Family member, please note that your personal data is also processed in accordance with the [IKEA Family Privacy Notice](https://www.ikea.com/se/en/customer-service/privacy-policy/ikea-family-integritetspolicy-pub66b5f5b0/). The IKEA Family Privacy Notice covers private customers who have an IKEA Family account. It explains how and why we collect, store, process and share any of the personal data you provide us when you use your IKEA Family membership in-store, online via our website and the IKEA App, on the second-hand marketplace and when you have contact with our customer support centres.

*   3\. What personal data is collected by IKEA?
    
    We collect your personal data when you, for example, buy products, use services or contact customer service.. Examples of personal data are full name, address, email address, phone number, personal identity number, payment details, purchase, order and usage history, IP address and other case-related information (e.g. information you provide when contacting our customer service). 
    

*   4\. Why does IKEA process your personal data?
    
    Once you have provided personal data to us at IKEA, we use this data for the purposes and on the legal basis set out below: 
    
    ### A. To be able to manage the order and administration of your purchase
    
    The contract of sales that we plan to enter into, or have entered into with you, constitutes the legal basis for the processing and is necessary for us to be able to meet our contractual obligations and fulfil the agreement towards you, e.g. for us to deliver your order, get paid, make a refund, use our planning tools or contact you to complete an order that has been started. 
    
    ### B. To be able to administer benefits linked to the IKEA Business Network and for the safe and correct handling of contact persons' personal data linked to the IKEA Business Network 
    
    The contract that we have entered into with you (i.e. our commitments in the IKEA Business Network customer terms and conditions) is the legal basis for processing business data and personal data such as personal identity numbers, if the company uses a corporate form where this applies as the organisation number or personal data linked to designated contact persons representing the company. The processing is necessary to enable us to fulfil our contractual obligations and administer benefits. 
    
    #### **To save card details**
    
    The legal basis is our legitimate interest to provide you with a convenient and secure shopping experience by enabling you to save your card details for future purchases. By selecting to save your card details, you allow IKEA to store them with our online Payment Service Provider. Card details are retained until expiry, deletion of the saved card, or closure of your IKEA account. It is your responsibility to maintain the security of your IKEA account and password, and to restrict access to your account.
    
    ### C. To be able to handle customer service enquiries
    
    IKEA has a legitimate interest that constitute the legal basis and the processing is necessary to be able to help you with questions about products or complaints related to our products and may also include follow-up questions and surveys on how you think products and services have worked for you as an IKEA customer. If your query concerns a complaint, we will process your data in order to fulfil our obligations under applicable consumer law and we will process your data on the legal basis of our contract of sales with you. 
    
    #### **To improve customer service**
    
    During your experience as an IKEA customer, you may encounter the use of AI, for example when you chat with us or call our customer service. If AI is used in connection with your personal data, we will clearly inform you through information notices at the time of collection.
    
    We use an AI assistant to answer common questions when you contact us. If the AI assistant cannot help or if you request to speak with a co-worker, your call will be transferred to a co-worker. The use of AI is based on our legitimate interest in improving customer service and reducing waiting times. The personal data processed may include your contact details, order information, and the questions you ask during your interaction with customer service.
    
    We also, if you do not opt out when calling us, record calls for training and quality purposes based on our legitimate interest. The transcription of the call may be analyzed using AI technology to improve your customer experience. You always have the option to opt out of recording and AI analysis if you wish.
    
    Chat conversations may, with your consent, be analyzed using AI technology to streamline our service and improve the quality of our products and services. You always have the option to opt out having your chat analyzed by AI if you wish. 
    
    ### D. To be able to send general marketing (such as sending our brochures) and personalised direct marketing via email, SMS, post, telephone or social media 
    
    The contract we have entered into with you (i.e. IKEA Business Network Terms & Condition and IKEA Företagsfaktura Terms & Condition) is the legal basis for processing the personal data. The processing is necessary for us to be able to fulfil our contractual obligations towards you, e.g. to make offers of products or services that we believe you/company may be interested in. If you are a customer of IKEA but not a member of the IKEA Family or if your company does not have an IKEA Business Network account or IKEA Företagsfaktura, IKEA will process your personal data based on our legitimate interest to market IKEA products and services. 
    
    ### E. To be able to carry out your participation in competitions and events 
    
    The legal basis is our legitimate interest to be able to administer or follow up on competitions and events such as confirmation of entries or questions or information about winners. 
    
    ### F. To be able to use, update and improve services and products and the technical functionality and security of our data systems
    
    The legal basis is our legitimate interest to be able to use and update the technology that enables you to visit our website, use our mobile applications or shop in our web shop or to understand how you as a customer experience our products and thereby improve existing and develop new products and services. It is also our legitimate interest to provide secure services, by preventing attempts at unauthorised access or unauthorised use of our services. 
    
    When you use in-store navigation in the IKEA app (for example, maps and wayfinding inside the store), we use location-based technology and SDKs and process location and sensor data from your device based on your consent, in order to show your position, guide you through the store, and improve your experience. If you are signed in with your IKEA Family account and provide specific consent in the app, we may also combine navigation data with information about your purchases to analyze how the service is used and optimize our in-store solutions. For guests and users who are not signed in, we use pseudonymous identifiers that are not used to directly identify you.
    
    ### G.  To be able to register those people who indicate that they do not want to receive unaddressed marketing from IKEA and are not registered as IKEA customers 
    
    Legitimate interest, at the request of an individual, not to receive marketing communications. 
    
    ### H.  In order to fulfil the company's legal obligations, such as those relating to product safety, governmental decisions or accounting requirements, or tax laws 
    
    The legal basis is to fulfil our legal obligations under the law. 
    
    ### I. Improving our customer meeting places 
    
    For logged-in customers, we collect ​​data on your use of our different channels to better understand how our customers use and move between channels (app, web, store). The legal basis for such processing is our legitimate interest to review and improve our customer meeting places. 
    
    ### J. Improved Marketing Experience 
    
    With your consent, we will use cookies and other similar technologies to collect user behavior on our website in order to, among other things, determine how we can display more relevant products for you. Read more about our cookie usage here.  
    
    ### K.  To be able to administer functions linked to IKEA Företagsfaktura and for secure and correct handling of contact persons' personal data linked to IKEA Företagsfaktura 
    
    The contract that we have entered into with you (i.e. our commitments in the IKEA Företagsfaktura Terms and Conditions) is the legal basis for processing business data and personal data such as personal identity number, if the company uses a corporate form where this applies as the corporate identity number or personal data linked to designated contact persons representing the company. The processing is necessary for us to fulfil our contractual obligations. 
    

*   5\. Updating name and address information
    
    IKEA processes personal identity numbers to ensure that we have accurate information about our customers. We automatically update your name and address details from SPAR (the Swedish state personal address register). We do this to ensure that the benefits and offers provided are linked to the right member. 
    

*   6\. How IKEA uses information about you as a member of IKEA Business Network
    
    When you order or purchase a product or service from us at IKEA, when you are a registered member of the  IKEA Business Network, we will use your purchase and order history and user history (e.g. how you click or search on our website) to gain an insight into and understand which products or services you are interested in and whether there are other similar products or services that we think you would also like. Based on this information, we at IKEA will be able to inform you about new additions to our range but also about timeless classics that you haven't yet discovered. We may also send you special benefits based on your/your company's interests and preferences and other similar mailings. This information is necessary for us to be able to provide you as a member with additional offers on top of what we offer to other customers. 
    
    If you do not want us to use your customer history to send you offers and other member benefits, you always have the right to unsubscribe from such mailings. If you don't want us to use your customer history at all (e.g. when developing and improving our products and services or improving technical functionality), you always have the right to cancel your IKEA Business Network membership. 
    
    The customer information that we store about you is based on data that IKEA has collected about you either from you or from official registers such as SPAR to ensure correct address details or other actors that IKEA cooperates with to create the collected information about you. 
    

*   7\. When we may share your data
    
    IKEA only shares your personal data when this is a legal requirement or otherwise permitted by law. In some cases, we may use a data processor to help us with our marketing or to process contracts and orders. Companies that help us in these cases can be freight forwarding and logistics companies, bank and credit card companies or marketing suppliers. In such cases, we have entered into a contract with the data processor that ensures there are security measures in place to protect your data. When we share your data, it will be used for the same purposes for which we originally collected it. 
    
    IKEA minimises the transfer of personal data to a country outside the EU/EEA and where this occurs, such as in the case of system support and maintenance, this is done according to particularly stringent requirements and agreements. 
    
    We also share your personal data with certain companies that are independent data controllers, which means that they control how the information is processed, such as public authorities or companies offering independent payment solutions or general goods transport. 
    

*   8\. Storage and retention time
    
    At IKEA, we process information about you as a customer in order to provide the best service and we keep your data for as long as the data is needed to fulfil the purposes for which the data was originally collected. The information can also be stored for as long as required under applicable law. We at IKEA have ongoing procedures to ensure that we do not store unnecessary data about you, and we minimise the retention time as much as possible. 
    
    **Customer information for purchases and customer service enquiries** 
    
    *   With regard to your purchase, order and usage history, to the extent that we have not anonymised your data, we will delete such history 10 years after the transaction date. 
    *   Data related to a customer service case is stored until it is closed or otherwise deleted after 8 years from the completion of the purchase transaction, if the case is linked to a purchase. 
    *   Regarding purchase history, the information will be used for marketing of similar IKEA products and services, for a retention period of 1 year. You can always unsubscribe from such mailings from IKEA. 
    
    **Information collected after consent**
    
    If you are neither a buyer nor a member of the IKEA Family or IKEA Business Network, but for example visit IKEA.se or on other occasions where it becomes relevant, IKEA will obtain consent from you before IKEA makes any registration of your personal data. IKEA keeps the information collected from you with your consent as long as it is relevant, but deletes historical information on an ongoing basis, 5 years from the time the information was collected. You can always withdraw your consent and the information will be deleted as soon as possible and at the latest within 6 months. 
    
    **Information regarding customers with IKEA Business Network or IKEA Företagsfaktura** 
    
    As a member of IKEA Business Network or a customer with IKEA Företagsfaktura, IKEA will keep relevant and up-to-date data related to your membership/account for as long as you choose to be a member/have a company invoice with us. If you no longer wish to be a member/cancel your business account, we will delete your data no later than 6 months after receiving your notice of cancellation, unless the data must be processed to comply with legal requirements. If you have been inactive (i.e. have not made any registered purchases or logged in to your member page) for 3 consecutive years (2 years for IKEA Företagsfaktura), we will delete your account and thus delete all personal data that is legally possible, about you or the company 
    

*   9\. Personal data security 
    
    At IKEA, we ensure that your personal data is handled according to specific security measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or improper access to the information. At IKEA, only the information that is needed is processed and only by people who need it, in order to provide the best service to our customers. 
    

*   10\. Your rights
    
    At IKEA, we always want to be transparent about how we process your data. If you wish to have access to your personal data processing, you have the right to request access to your data via a data subject access request. If we receive a request from you, we may ask for additional information to ensure that we are disclosing the data to the right person. Any request for an extract from the registry must be in writing, duly signed by you. You always have the right to request a rectification of your personal data. You can always log in to your user account to correct incorrect data. 
    
    You can request the deletion of your data to the extent consistent with applicable law and in accordance with the agreements entered into with you, and we will of course always do our best to fulfil your request for deletion. You also have the right to object to our processing (e.g. if any of the data collected is inaccurate). In some cases, IKEA may not be able to fulfil your request for erasure if the data is needed to comply with applicable law. 
    
    Your personal data may not be processed for direct marketing or profiling purposes if you object to such processing. You also have the right to withdraw your consent at any time and to refuse different types of marketing.

*   11\. Complaints and supervisory authority
    
    If you believe that we have processed your personal data incorrectly, you can always contact us at IKEA. The Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection is responsible for supervising data protection legislation and anyone who believes that personal data is being processed incorrectly can submit a complaint to [The Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection](https://www.imy.se/) 
    

*   12\. Contact us
    
    Our goal is to always provide you with simple and accurate information about how we use your personal data. At IKEA, the privacy of our customers is important to us, and we are constantly working on improvements so that you as a customer always feel safe when you provide us with your data. If you have any questions about how we use your personal data, you are welcome to contact our customer service via our contact form or [via our chat which you can find on our website](https://www.ikea.com/se/en/customer-service/contact-us/)
