# AI at The Home Depot: A Look at the Future of Home Improvement

The Home Depot currently offers more than a dozen AI-powered capabilities, with numerous others in development. Through new artificial intelligence tools, the company is delivering its home improvement expertise directly to millions of DIY customers and professional contractors. As **Angie Brown**, executive vice president and CIO, explains, "Everything we build is to improve the customer and associate experience."

#### **AI Tools for the DIYer**

The Home Depot is simplifying the DIY journey by providing personalized, real-time advice that makes even the most complex projects feel achievable, paired with more seamless AI search product discovery capabilities thanks to new technology integrations.

##### **Magic Apron & Outdoor Assistant**

This virtual expert brings orange-aproned knowledge to customers everywhere. With the [**Outdoor Assistant**](https://www.homedepot.com/b/Outdoors-Garden-Center/N-5yc1vZbx6k), snap a photo of a plant for immediate guidance on care, safety and sunlight.

Customers can now discover the latest The Home Depot product catalog in ChatGPT. Browse visually and refine results conversationally within the app, including side-by-side product comparisons to find the perfect fit.

#### **AI Behind the Scenes**

While tools like Magic Apron are highly visible, some of The Home Depot’s most impactful innovations happen where the customer **never sees them**. These "invisible" solutions are directly responsible for the speed and reliability of capabilities like **same-day** and [**next-day delivery**](https://www.homedepot.com/c/delivery)**.**

##### **Order Intelligence**

Currently live in more than a dozen facilities, this system leverages AI to identify and solve potential delivery misses **before** they ever impact the customer.

##### **Route & Load Optimization**

By partnering with [**Google**](https://corporate.homedepot.com/news/partnerships/home-depot-and-google-cloud-launch-agentic-ai-tools-help-customers-and-associates) and Microsoft, the company uses AI to optimize truck routes and loads, ensuring faster delivery and reducing the number of vehicles on the road.

#### **Putting People at the Heart of Technology**

At its core, The Home Depot’s AI transformation is an investment in enhancing the customer experience. **Jordan Broggi**, executive vice president of customer experience and president of online, notes that the true power of these tools is the time they return. “We’re a project business. Our goal with AI is to empower both our customers and our associates by removing friction from home improvement. We’re providing world-class tools that give time and expertise to our teams and confidence to our customers, allowing everyone to focus on what matters most: expert, human-to-human problem solving and bringing projects to life."
