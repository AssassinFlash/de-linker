# Shopify Help Center | Configuring chat settings and appearance for Shopify Inbox

This section covers your chat settings, the [customer sign-in setting](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/chat-settings-and-appearance/settings#customer-sign-in), instant answers, and automatic first replies. Your greeting, availability hours, and automatic first replies apply to conversations that your staff handle. The **Track my order** option is available regardless of whether your [Inbox agent](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inbox/agent-persona) is active. When the Inbox agent is assigned to conversations, it uses its own persona and configuration, which you can manage from the [**Inbox**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-inbox) page in your Shopify admin.

From your Shopify admin, in the [Inbox channel](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-inbox), you can manage your chat settings, instant answers, and first replies.

From your Shopify admin, in the [theme editor](https://admin.shopify.com/themes), you can turn on chat for your online store and customize the appearance and placement of your chat button and greeting message.
