# About Rufus - Amazon Customer Service

Find more solutions

Rufus is an AI-powered shopping experience that lets you ask all kinds of shopping questions in the Amazon Shopping app and on Amazon.co.uk.

## To get started with Rufus:

1.  Go to Amazon.co.uk and on the top left side of the navigation bar you’ll find the Rufus button.
2.  Select the Rufus button, and a chat window will appear on the bottom left corner of the page.
3.  With the Rufus chat window open, you can ask a question or choose one of the suggested questions.
4.  When you’re done, you can click the X button to close the chat window or the minimize button to drop it down the lower left-hand corner of the page.

## Can I still use search as I always have?

Yes. You can still search by typing a product name or keywords in the search bar. Rufus and search work together to answer shopping questions and find relevant products.

## How can I provide feedback?

Rufus is an AI-powered shopping experience and may not always get things right. Let us know how it's doing by giving a thumbs up or thumbs down.
