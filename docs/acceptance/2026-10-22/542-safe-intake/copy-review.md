# Intake state copy review

Scope: customer-facing form outcomes and native staff controls. The dimension is
submission recovery and truthful saved state, using the existing Inquiry/Anfrage
vocabulary. This is operational form copy; no product/service claim is added.

## Draft and review

1. The draft called all stored records “received” and described failed proof even
   when an already verified campaign sample was held. Rejected: it implied a team
   handoff and misstated the reason for verified campaign samples.
2. The draft described aggregate activity as an acknowledgement. Rejected: only a
   bounded counter exists beyond the three raw samples. It must explicitly say no
   separate inquiry or email was created and keep the original editable input.
3. The draft described internal notifications in customer receipt prose. Revised:
   “saved and waiting for review” plus “no confirmation email” tells the customer
   the decision-relevant state. Staff-only controls retain the queue/job detail.
4. The draft reused “same original notification” for a new intake whose job is 0.
   Revised: staff sees that one internal notification will be queued after release,
   while the old #537 wording remains for a retained original message.
5. Hard Stops checked: no borrowed authority, invented guarantee, reply-time claim,
   marketing negation, logistics or model terminology. Review outcome does not
   promise delivery, spam accuracy or immediate staff attention. EN source and
   formal DE independently checked for state meaning and existing Anfrage terms.
