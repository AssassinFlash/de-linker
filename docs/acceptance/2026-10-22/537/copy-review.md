# Internal notification hold copy review

Scope: internal staff controls only, EN plus independent formal DE. Customer forms, messages, routing and model labels do not change.

Draft: “Notification blocked / Unblock and send.”

Adversarial review: “blocked” suggests the inquiry was rejected; “send” promises immediate delivery despite a full throttle. Neither states the retained original message. A model-observation explanation saying every inquiry is notified conflicts with an explicit staff notification hold.

Final: “Notification on hold”; “The inquiry and its original notification are retained. Releasing the notification queues the same message for delivery.”; “Release this notification”; resulting state “Released for delivery”. Diagnostic labels distinguish hold reason, UTC time and original notification ID. The model explanation now states that model labels do not reject inquiries or stop team notifications.
