# Staff assessment wording review

Scope: internal team notification and native Woo staff screen. The customer form, receipt, approval wording and public marketing copy are unchanged. Ruler: linker-copywriting Hard Stops and AGENTS terminology. No product/stock/performance claim is added; no customer identity or provider free text becomes a generated allegation.

## Draft

Normal / Spam / Unknown; model result; manual override.

## Adversarial critique

“Normal” and “Spam” sound definitive, although the 60-call pilot only measures synthetic feasibility. “Unknown” conceals timeout and operational failures. “Override” suggests deleting model evidence. The staff screen must explain that all inquiries still reach the team and preserve the raw original choices next to a separate human correction. Do not imply a confidence score predicts the real false-positive rate.

## Final English

Jev initial assessment · Likely genuine · Suspected spam · Unable to assess. Initial guidance only. Every inquiry is accepted and notified. Unknown cases: “No reliable assessment was available. Please review the inquiry.” Separate controls: Staff correction / Review note / Save correction. Fixed evidence reasons describe a possible purpose, not a proven accusation. Provider errors are fixed diagnostic codes.

## Independently written German

Ersteinschätzung durch Jev · Vermutlich echte Anfrage · Spamverdacht · Keine Beurteilung möglich. Die Einschätzung dient als Hinweis. Jede Anfrage wird gespeichert und dem Team gemeldet. Eine verlässliche Einschätzung liegt nicht vor. Bitte prüfen Sie die Anfrage. Manuelle Korrektur / Prüfvermerk / Korrektur speichern.

German uses Sie where addressing staff, existing Anfrage terminology, and natural short labels. Both locales were rendered in the actual mail template and staff screen at phone/desktop widths. Team email retains the existing site's default locale; German template QA temporarily overrides that locale inside a local CLI process only. German submission does not change the internal recipient's language rule.
