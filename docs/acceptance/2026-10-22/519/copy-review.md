# Protection copy review

Scope: native staff health, the verification-only diagnostic and shared failure
messages. English is the source; German formal was written for the same states.
No product claims, access promises or automatic spam decisions are introduced.

## Draft and adversarial pass

- “Request failed” was insufficient after an uncertain side effect: it could
  encourage a duplicate inquiry. Final pending/completed-replay error asks the
  customer to check the request status before sending it again.
- “Protection enabled” overstated the shared helper's coverage. Final health
  page separates mode, key readiness and explicitly declared native handlers.
  With no consumers it states that none are connected.
- A successful diagnostic could look like a sent inquiry. The final EN/DE result
  explicitly says verification passed and no business request was submitted.
- “Total requests/cost” would misdescribe the bounded journal. Final copy says
  these are receipts within the retry window and not full traffic/exact costs.
- Vendor bodies are not customer text. All shared error messages are fixed,
  localized and omit tokens, private keys, raw inputs and upstream diagnostics.

## Final

The PHP PO/MO and native WP-CLI-generated script JSON contain the reviewed EN/DE
messages. Staff strings use Sie and do not ask the visitor to re-enter or restore
a password. The form consumer must keep the customer's fields on every refusal;
that behavior is tested in the shared client and connected in #520/#521.
Independent full-diff Spec/Standards review is recorded separately.
