## Remaining P2: commercial cover navigation contrast

The language rail fix does not fix every white header element on a bright photograph. In [Commercial EN1440 top](cua-local-20261022T0602/final-header-commercial-en-1440-top.jpg), the small white primary navigation still sits directly over the pale gray upper photo. The same appearance is visible in the DE desktop top view. Base chrome sets ordinary navigation to 14.5px/600; the cover state makes it white. This is not introduced by the selector extension, but it prevents granting the full commercial photo header a readability PASS.

For reproducibility, the untouched EN JPEG was read through a temporary BMP representation and nearby text-free photo pixels sampled at x190–600/y32–33. Background values are approximately RGB `(139–146,146–153,154–161)`, with white-to-background ratios **2.88–3.15:1** at the sampled locations. This is an approximate rendered-JPEG background analysis, not an exact DOM glyph-pixel or full accessibility certification. It agrees with the visibly pale small navigation text. Original SHA, method, coordinates and values are saved privately in `commercial-nav-contrast-observed.json` under the reviewer directory. The producer was notified immediately.

This remaining header state needs a concrete contrast correction and fresh EN/DE cover readback before overall photo-header acceptance. A repaired language capsule alone cannot close that broader visual defect. No source was changed by the reviewer to address it.
