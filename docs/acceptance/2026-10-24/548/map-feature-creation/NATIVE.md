## Browser touch events, separate from hardware acceptance

The in-app browser CDP port did not support touch dispatch. Chrome's documented
CDP capability did; a temporary Chrome tab used the same existing test customer
and p548 project at final pin, 390 × 844. These are browser-native emulated
touch events, not a physical handset claim. A pre-existing browser extension's
pink decoration remains in the original Chrome images; it is not product UI.

1. A touch tap opened Map. In a new, deliberately unsubmitted keep-out drawing,
   one tap recorded one corner.
2. A single-finger pan changed the view without adding a corner. A two-finger
   pinch visibly zoomed the actual map without adding a corner. Fit restored
   the point to its previous displayed position, still one corner.
3. Pause/reload/reopen retained one corner. Two more touch taps produced two,
   then three corners; Close became available and opened the real Terra editor.
4. A single-finger drag moved the first handle from `(220,380)` to `(215,370)`.
   A second finger introduced during the next drag interrupted editing and
   paused the drawing. Resume/Fit retained the previous completed handle
   position and three corners. The independently captured complete PNGs before
   this interrupted drag and after resumption are **byte-identical**, as the
   hashes in `native-final-checks.json` show. This visual equality is not a
   claim about private journal bytes or measurement accuracy.
5. Discarded only this newly created unsubmitted diagnostic drawing through its
   normal UI. Server readback remains exact v14. Touch emulation and viewport
   override were reset, the Chrome customer session was signed out through the
   normal account menu, and the temporary tab closed.
