## 1. What backup rating does GW30K-ETA-G20 have?

**Status:** pass
**Review:** pass

**EN Q:** What backup rating does GW30K-ETA-G20 have?

On the AC side (Back-up Port) the rated apparent power is 30 kVA. The maximum apparent power is 33 kVA off-grid (45.0 kVA for 10 s) and 55.2 kVA on-grid.

**DE Q:** Welche Notstromwerte hat der GW30K-ETA-G20?

Auf der AC-Seite (Back-up-Anschluss) beträgt die Nennscheinleistung 30 kVA. Die maximale Scheinleistung beträgt 33 kVA im Off-Grid-Betrieb (45,0 kVA für 10 s) und 55,2 kVA im On-Grid-Betrieb.

**EN Q:** How many MPPTs does GW30K-ETA-G20 have?

The GW30K-ETA-G20 has 4 MPPTs, with 1/1/2/2 strings per MPPT.

## 3. Which battery modules stack with this power module?

**Review:** pass

**EN Q:** Which battery modules stack with this power module?

The battery system models are GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 and GW9.0-BAT-D-G20. Battery modules of different models can be mixed. The system supports 5-108kWh.

**DE Q:** Welche Batteriemodule gehören zu diesem Leistungsmodul?

Die Batteriesystem-Modelle sind GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 und GW9.0-BAT-D-G20. Batteriemodule unterschiedlicher Modelle können gemischt werden. Das System unterstützt 5-108kWh.
