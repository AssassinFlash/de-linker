**Status:** withdrawn
**Review:** withdrawn — The retrieved passages carry scalability statements (power expansion, capacity expansion, hybrid use of old and new batteries, isolation of failed battery modules) and the parallel limits of 3 junction boxes with a maximum of 6 battery expansion modules per junction box. **EN Q:** What should I record when planning an older tower extension?

The passages do not name any data to record for planning an extension of an older tower. They state that the battery system supports power expansion, battery capacity expansion, and hybrid use of old and new batteries, and that failed battery modules can be isolated so the energy storage system can still operate normally. A separate passage states that up to 3 junction boxes can be connected in parallel and one junction box supports a maximum of 6 battery expansion modules.

Eine weitere Passage gibt an, dass bis zu 3 Anschlusskästen parallel geschaltet werden können und ein Anschlusskasten maximal sechs Batterieerweiterungsmodule unterstützt.
