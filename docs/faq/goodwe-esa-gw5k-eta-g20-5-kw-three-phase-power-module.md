## 3. Which battery modules stack with this power module?

**Status:** pass
**Review:** pass

**EN Q:** Which battery modules stack with this power module?

The manual lists the battery modules GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 and GW9.0-BAT-D-G20, and states that battery modules of different models can be mixed. For stacking, the manual gives these limits: GW5.1-BAT-D-G20 and GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 up to 3 batteries, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20 and GW8.3-BAT-D-G21 up to 2 batteries, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 up to 3 batteries, GW9.0-BAT-D-G20 up to 2 batteries. When GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 and GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 are mixed and stacked, each system group supports up to 3 batteries. For floor installation, up to 4 batteries can be stacked away from the wall and up to 6 batteries against the wall.

**DE Q:** Welche Batteriemodule gehören zu diesem Leistungsmodul?

Das Handbuch nennt die Batteriemodule GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 und GW9.0-BAT-D-G20 und gibt an, dass Batteriemodule verschiedener Modelle gemischt werden können. Für die Stapelung nennt das Handbuch folgende Grenzen: GW5.1-BAT-D-G20 und GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 maximal 3 Batterien, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20 und GW8.3-BAT-D-G21 maximal 2 Batterien, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 maximal 3 Batterien, GW9.0-BAT-D-G20 maximal 2 Batterien. Bei gemischter Stapelung von GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 und GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 unterstützt jede Systemgruppe maximal 3 Batterien. Bei der Bodenmontage können maximal 4 Batterien von der Wand entfernt und maximal 6 Batterien an der Wand gestapelt werden.
