## 1. What backup rating does GW3K-EHA-G20 have?

**Status:** pass
**Review:** pass

**EN Q:** What backup rating does GW3K-EHA-G20 have?

The GW3K-EHA-G20 has a nominal output apparent power of 3 kVA on the backup side. The maximum output apparent power is 3.0 kVA (6.0 kVA for 10 s), and the maximum output apparent power in bypass is 6 kVA.

**DE Q:** Welche Notstromwerte hat der GW3K-EHA-G20?

Der GW3K-EHA-G20 hat eine nominale Ausgangsscheinleistung von 3 kVA auf der Notstromseite. Die maximale Ausgangsscheinleistung beträgt 3,0 kVA (6,0 kVA für 10 s), und die maximale Ausgangsscheinleistung im Bypass beträgt 6 kVA.

## 3. Which battery modules stack with this power module?

**Status:** reject
**Review:** reject — The cited p42 table lists the battery models and the mixed-use note, but the answer's phrasing 'can be stacked with the power module' is not carried by the passage, which describes coupling scenario equipment, not stacking compatibility with this specific power module; the stacking rules are on p91/p46.

**EN Q:** Which battery modules stack with this power module?

The following battery modules can be stacked with the power module: GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20, and GW9.0-BAT-D-G20. Battery modules of different models support mixed use.

**DE Q:** Welche Batteriemodule gehören zu diesem Leistungsmodul?

Folgende Batteriemodule können mit dem Leistungsmodul gestapelt werden: GW5.1-BAT-D-G20, GW5.1-BAT-D-G21, GW8.3-BAT-D-G20, GW8.3-BAT-D-G21, GW6.0-BAT-D-G20 und GW9.0-BAT-D-G20. Batteriemodule unterschiedlicher Modelle können gemischt verwendet werden.
