Description

GoodWe GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 (ESA battery module, 5.12 kWh)

GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 is the 5.12 kWh rated ESA battery module with 5.0 kWh usable under the printed test. Mass 57.5 ± 1 kg, 800 × 326 × 270 mm. Charge from +2 °C; no heating film. This designation is on the English ESA datasheet and the manuals; it is not a column on the EMEA German ESA datasheet or the EMEA Lynx D G2 datasheet. No EU battery-warranty document in hand names G21.

  • 5.0 kWh usable under the printed test; 57.5 ± 1 kg; IP66.

  • Aerosol fire extinguishing (Integrated); no heating.

A 57.5 ± 1 kg module

The module weighs 57.5 ± 1 kg and measures 800 × 326 × 270 mm. Mounting: Floor stacked / Wall-mounted.

Functions in the module

Aerosol fire extinguishing (Integrated); no heating. IP66. Charging +2 ~ +55 °C.

Product questions

Does GW5.1-BAT-D-G21 include heating?

Aerosol fire extinguishing (Integrated); no heating. Charging temperature range +2 ~ +55 °C; discharging −20 ~ +55 °C.

How many modules in one column?

Up to six modules in one column and twelve in the system. Different BAT-D models can be mixed; mixing 5.1/8.3 G20 or G21 with 6.0/9.0 G20 disables battery heating.
