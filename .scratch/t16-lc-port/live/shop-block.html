Batch item-number import

Rebuild a quotation or an old order in one paste. Matching against our products works right away and matched items go into your current Project; prices and online ordering open after account review.

What it accepts
  • One item per line, quantity after a comma, semicolon or Tab: DLK-GOODWE-GW15KETAG20, 4
  • Pasting two columns straight from Excel works; the Tab is understood.
  • Item numbers as printed on product pages and quotations, with or without the DLK- prefix. Case does not matter.
  • A line without a quantity counts as 1. A quantity that cannot be read is reported on its line rather than changed.
